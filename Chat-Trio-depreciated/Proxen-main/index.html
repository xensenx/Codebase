About the AI

The AI companion isn't a cheerleader. It's not a therapist. It's not trying to be your friend.

Think of it as a tired but competent colleague who helps you track what needs doing. Slightly dry. Occasionally sarcastic. Always focused.

It will acknowledge your progress without making it feel like a participation trophy.

Early-Stage Project: v1.3. Proxen does not collect or transmit data to any Proxen server. It cannot protect against compromised browsers, malicious extensions, or stolen devices.

Technical Details & Data Flow

Data Flow

Your Browser
↓ HTTPS
Google AI API
↓ HTTPS
Your Browser

Your browser makes a direct HTTPS request to Google's API. The response returns directly to your browser. Proxen's servers are not part of this chain.

What Proxen Does Not Do

  • Does not log requests or responses
  • Does not store messages on any server
  • Does not forward data to third parties
  • Does not perform analytics or telemetry
  • Does not use your data for fine-tuning or training
  • Does not create user accounts or profiles
  • Does not track you across sessions or devices

Local Storage Details

Proxen stores the following in your browser's localStorage:

  • API Key: Your Google API key (stored by browser, not Proxen)
  • Username: The name you provide for personalization
  • Tasks: Your task list and completion status
  • Conversation History: Last 6 messages for context (local only)

Verification

  • Open browser DevTools (F12) → Network tab
  • Confirm all API requests go to generativelanguage.googleapis.com
  • Review source code at github.com/xensenx/Proxen
  • Inspect localStorage: DevTools → Application → Local Storage

Privacy & Data Handling

What Data Is Stored

Proxen stores the following on your device only:

  • Your Google API key
  • Your chosen username
  • Your task list
  • Recent conversation context (last 6 messages)

What Is Never Collected

  • Email addresses or contact information
  • IP addresses or location data
  • Usage analytics or telemetry
  • Device fingerprints or browsing history

How to Remove All Data

  1. Click "Reset" in the header, or
  2. Clear browser localStorage in DevTools, or
  3. Clear site data for this domain

Security Model

What Proxen Protects Against

  • Server breaches: There are no Proxen servers to breach
  • Account database leaks: There are no user accounts
  • Third-party tracking: No analytics or tracking scripts
  • Data mining: Proxen cannot access or analyze your data
  • Cross-device profiling: Data never leaves your device

What Proxen Does Not Protect Against

  • Compromised browsers: All local data is at risk
  • Malicious extensions: Can read localStorage and intercept requests
  • Stolen devices: Physical access = data access
  • Network interception: HTTPS protects, compromised networks may pose risks
  • Google API vulnerabilities: Proxen relies on Google's security
